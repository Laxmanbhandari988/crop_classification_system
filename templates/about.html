About Our System

The Crop Recommendation System is an AI-powered platform designed to help farmers and agricultural professionals make informed decisions about crop selection.

Our system analyzes various environmental and soil parameters including nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium levels, temperature, humidity, pH, and rainfall to recommend the most suitable crops for cultivation.

Key Features:

  • Machine Learning Powered: Uses Random Forest algorithm trained on extensive agricultural data
  • 22 Crop Types: Covers a wide range of crops from cereals to fruits and pulses
  • User-Friendly Interface: Easy-to-use web interface for quick predictions
  • History Tracking: Keep track of all your predictions and recommendations
  • Detailed Crop Information: Learn about ideal growing conditions for each crop

Our Mission

To empower farmers with data-driven insights and help optimize agricultural productivity through technology. We believe that by providing accurate crop recommendations, we can contribute to sustainable farming practices and improved yields.
